# architectural

## architectural boundary

An architectural boundary in software engineering is the line or barrier that separates different parts of a software system to keep them independent of each other.
Book Clean Architecture, boundaries are designed to prevent business logic from depending on technical details, such as databases, user interfaces, or third-party frameworks.
